ClipForge Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 28, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how ClipForge handles information when used to prepare and publish creator-owned short-form video content.

2. Information processed

ClipForge may process source video files, clip files, captions, hashtags, publish status data, and platform authorization tokens needed to post content on the creator's behalf.

3. How information is used

Information is used only to generate clips, prepare publishing metadata, authenticate with connected platforms, and submit or track posts requested by the creator.

4. Token handling

Platform access tokens and refresh tokens are stored locally by the application operator and are used solely to make authorized API requests to connected platforms.

5. Sharing

ClipForge does not sell personal information. Information is shared only with the connected platform APIs required to authenticate, upload, or publish content.

6. Retention

Locally stored files, metadata, and tokens are retained only as long as needed for content production, scheduling, troubleshooting, or compliance with connected platform workflows.

7. Security

Reasonable steps are taken to store locally managed tokens and content securely, but no method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. User control

The creator may revoke platform access, delete local token files, and remove local content files at any time.

9. Third-party services

Connected platforms such as TikTok and YouTube may independently collect and process information according to their own privacy policies.

10. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact the operator of ClipForge through the official Musikage contact channels.
