ClipForge (Musikage)

Creator workflow for turning long-form music videos into short-form posts.

ClipForge is a creator-owned desktop workflow used to turn finished long-form videos into short clips, generate titles and hashtags, and post those clips to platforms such as YouTube Shorts and TikTok.

What it does

Downloads or imports source videos, detects clip boundaries, formats clips vertically, and prepares post metadata.

Who it is for

Used by the creator behind Musikage to publish original remix and game/anime music content more efficiently.

TikTok use case

Lets the creator explicitly authorize TikTok posting, then posts creator-owned short clips with captions and hashtags.

How TikTok integration works

  • The creator signs in with TikTok and grants permission through TikTok Login Kit.
  • ClipForge stores tokens locally for the creator's own account only.
  • When the creator schedules or posts a short, ClipForge sends the video and caption to TikTok using TikTok's official Content Posting API.
  • ClipForge then checks post status and records the result locally.
